"Act as a Senior Agricultural Logistics Advisor specializing in rice production, storage, and distribution systems in Southern Mindanao.

Context: Rice producers are experiencing delays in the movement of harvested palay due to severe weather conditions, temporary road access issues, and bottlenecks in storage and transport networks. These disruptions are affecting the timely delivery of rice from farming communities to drying facilities, warehouses, rice mills, and local markets.
Format: Output in clear Markdown with exactly three actionable steps under the heading '### Emergency Interventions'."
